# DefendOpenScience

Open science is currently facing significant threats.
Across global research landscapes, we are seeing a push to redefine "Openness" and to reconsider to whom we open our data (including source code). 

The arguments usually involves two main points:

* Systemic Rivalry & Geopolitics: Governments are increasingly arguing for "Closed Science", "Trusted Openness" or "As open as possible" to prevent
  technology transfer to geopolitical rivals.
* The Big Tech: The feeling of becoming powerless in the face of "Big Tech" that absorb community created data and code, ignoring licenses and copyright and selling "intelligence".

But not only that, AI slop, aggressive bots and accelerated AI-enabled cybersecurity incidents are challenging infrastructures, services and Open Source communities.
